BOTH

Shared ground

Try-on hauls and fit checks are the follower engine: women save them, men rewatch them. The shop slots turn that reach into money.

  • Your existing car, mirror and OOTD clips still work as Night Vibe remixes
  • Comment bait: “which fit?”, “rate it 1–10”, “guys, is this true?”, polls

What “winning” looks like

  • 2-sec view rate above 70% means the hook works. Below 50%, change the first frame and the text overlay, not the whole idea.
  • Saves above 0.5% of views on Shop Drops means she'll buy. Make more in that category. The 11 competitor hits we measured ran 0.12–0.69%.
  • Shares and rewatches on try-ons mean that format is pulling new followers in. Make a Pt. 2 within 48 hours.
  • Every Sunday, pick the top 3 posts of the week and remake each one with a new hook in the coming week.

Getting into TikTok Shop

  • Affiliate access requires the account holder to be 18+ and to meet a follower minimum (5,000 in the US for a long time). Check Creator Center for the current number.
  • Until you're eligible, still post the Shop Drop slot as styling content. It teaches the algorithm who her buyers are, and you'll know which products work before you can link them.
  • Once you're approved, request free samples. Real b-roll of the actual product (fabric, clasp, inside the bag) next to Tiff makes the videos more honest and more convincing.

Rules that keep the account alive

This part protects everything else. TikTok requires realistic AI content to be labeled, and US law (the FTC's 2024 rule on fake reviews) treats AI-generated testimonials about products as fake reviews. AI shop accounts that ignore this get their reach restricted or lose shop access.
  • Turn on the “AI-generated content” label on every post, and say “AI creator” in the bio. Lean into it; it's part of the brand.
  • No first-person experience claims. Not “I've worn these all month” or “softest thing I own”. Say “the one with the sunburst buckle” or “comes in 6 colors”. Take features from the listing.
  • No AI before/after results (hair tools, skincare, shapewear). Show the product, not a made-up result.
  • In Shop Drops, show the real product (seller photos or sample footage) alongside Tiff.
  • No “dupe of [brand]” language, no fake brand logos, and only quote sale prices you've confirmed in the listing.
  • Fitted, tight and gym outfits are fine and are the point. Avoid sexualized camera framing (lingering body crops, suggestive poses/captions): TikTok cuts reach on it and can pull shop access.
  • Pre-workout / supplements: show her using it, quote only the label (flavor, servings). No energy, fat-loss or performance claims, and check the category is open to your affiliate account.
