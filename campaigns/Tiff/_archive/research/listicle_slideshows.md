- **The text is tiny and sits in the middle.** There's no big hook banner, so the vibe sells it and the words feel like a diary.
- Every slide has a short, calm imperative of 4–10 words. They aren't hot takes.
- She mixes 2 selfies with 4 lifestyle POV/object shots. The face doesn't appear on every slide.
- The light is low and moody throughout: dusk, blue hour, candles, train window.
- The caption is only hashtags: #staycalm #stayfocused #deepthoughts #overthink #silence #mindsetmotivation #relatable #fyp.

## Template for Tiff
- **6–7 slides.** Selfies go on slides 1, 3 and 6. The others are Codex moody b-roll per [[carousel-vibe-broll]]: matcha, candle, car at dusk, POV feet, ocean at blue hour.
- **Slide 1 is a selfie with the list title in small text**, e.g. "how i stay calm when everything's falling apart". Or, like Sofia, start straight with "1." and let the face be the hook.
- Text: lowercase or sentence case, white, small, centered, no background box. Add it in post, not in the image gen.
- Selfies should look serious or soft, not smiling, in low light (car, train window, mirror at night).
- Caption: hashtags only, or one short line. Sound: a trending moody or R&B original sound.
